Banlist generator

Pick the entities you would rather not be connected to and get the matching Bitcoin Core commands.

setban blocks an address in both directions: your node stops accepting connections from it and stops connecting to it. Some of the listed addresses are reachable nodes, so a ban can also cost you a peer you would otherwise connect to.

Many of the listed networks are whole ranges: banning a range also bans any unrelated node in it, and a determined entity can move to new addresses faster than this collection can follow. The data is centralized, maintained by hand, and incomplete.
